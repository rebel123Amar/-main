# 🎨 Rebel Custom App — Per-Product Customizer Interface Guide
### (App Ke Dashboard & Settings Se Har Product Ko Control Kaise Karein)

Ab aapko tags daalne ki bhi zaroorat nahi hai! Client **Rebel Custom App** ke interface ke andar se hi har product par alag-alag fields aur image limits set kar sakta hai.

---

## 📍 Step-by-Step: App Me Kahan Jana Hai?

1. **Shopify Admin** open karein.
2. Left sidebar me **Apps** ➔ **rebel-custom-app** open karein.
3. Navigation bar me **"Product Customizer"** par click karein (ya Settings page par jakar **"Manage Per-Product Customizer ➔"** par click karein).
4. Store ke saare products ki list thumbnail aur unke current status ke sath dikhegi!

---

## ⚡ 1-Click Presets (Sabse Fast Setup)

Kisi bhi product ke aage **"⚙️ Configure"** button par click karein. Aapko yeh 1-click options milenge:

| Preset Name | Best For (Product Example) | Website Par Kya Banega |
|---|---|---|
| 📷 **1 Photo** | Customized Mug, Keychain, Wallet Card, Acrylic Plaque | Sirf **1 Photo** upload + Text field (Pop-out image gayab). |
| 👥 **2 Photos** | Couple Frame, Double-Sided Keychain | Sirf **2 Photos** upload + Text field. |
| 🖼️ **Pop-Up Frame** | 3D Pop Up Collage Frames | **4 se 6 Photos** collage + **1 Pop-Out Center** photo + Text field. |
| ✍️ **Text / Engraving Only** | Name Jewellery, Ring, Bar Necklace, Bracelet | Photo upload poori tarah hide ho jayega, sirf **Text Box** dikhega! |
| 📸 **Photo Only** | Photo Canvas, Prints | Text box gayab ho jayega, sirf photo upload dikhega. |
| 🚫 **Disable Customizer** | Normal items (Stands, T-Shirts, Gift boxes) | Customizer band ho jayega, normal **Add to Cart** button chalega! |
| ⚙️ **Custom Setup** | Any special product | Apne hisaab se Min/Max photos (1 se 10), Text Labels, Pop-out toggles set karein. |

---

## 🛠️ Detailed Custom Controls (Agar Customize Karna Ho)

Agar preset ke alawa specific changes karne hon:

1. **Master Enable / Disable**:
   - Toggle ko band kar denge toh us product par customizer bilkul nahi aayega.
2. **Text Field Controls**:
   - Text Box Enable / Disable
   - Label change (e.g. *"Enter Name to Engrave"* ya *"Special Date"*)
   - Placeholder text (e.g. *"e.g. Rahul & Priya"*)
   - Make Mandatory (Required check)
3. **Photo Upload Controls**:
   - Photo Upload Enable / Disable
   - Section Label (e.g. *"Upload 1 Photo"* ya *"Select 4 Photos"*)
   - Min Photos Required (1 se 10 slider)
   - Max Photos Allowed (1 se 10 slider)
   - *(Note: Agar Max Photos 1 hai, toh nayi photo select karne par puraani photo apne aap replace ho jayegi)*
4. **Pop-Out Photo Controls**:
   - Pop-Out Photo Enable / Disable
   - Pop-Out Label
   - Make Pop-Out Mandatory

## 💾 Save & Preview
1. Settings choose karke **"Save Configuration"** par click karein.
2. Modal me **"View Product Storefront ↗"** ya list me **"👁️ View Live"** par click karke turant live website par check karein!
